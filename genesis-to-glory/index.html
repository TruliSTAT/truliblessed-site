Genesis to Glory: the animated Bible in a year

From the first “Let there be light” to the last page of Revelation, Genesis to Glory tells the whole Bible as one connected story. It is a weekly animated Bible series made for teens, families and adults who want to read the Bible in a year together. The premiere is Christmas Day, December 25, 2026.

“And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.” Genesis 1:3 KJV

Animated Bible stories with heart (and a little humor)

Pip the Lamb is brave, a little sarcastic and all heart. Lumi the Firefly is wise and witty, and she carries the light. Wren the Sparrow worries a lot and always has a plan. Together they walk through Creation, the patriarchs, the Exodus, the kings and prophets, the life of Jesus and the early church.

A Bible in a year plan for teens and families

  • Watch one short episode each week, in Bible order.
  • Read along with a simple daily reading plan from the King James Version.
  • Talk it over with a daily Bible verse and a question for the dinner table or group chat.
“Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.” Psalm 119:105 KJV
