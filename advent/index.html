The Family Advent Adventure

An Advent devotional for families with teens and kids. Starting Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, November 26, 2026, Pip the Lamb and Lumi the Firefly lead a daily countdown to Christmas Day and the premiere of A Light to Glory.

How it works

  1. Read one short KJV verse together each day.
  2. Ask one question around the table: “Where did you see light today?”
  3. Do one small act of kindness to let your light shine.

Week 1: Hope (from Nov 26)

“Arise, shine; for thy light is come, and the glory of the LORD is risen upon thee.” Isaiah 60:1 KJV

Week 2: Peace

“For unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given: and the government shall be upon his shoulder: and his name shall be called Wonderful, Counsellor, The mighty God, The everlasting Father, The Prince of Peace.” Isaiah 9:6 KJV

Week 3: Joy

“And the angel said unto them, Fear not: for, behold, I bring you good tidings of great joy, which shall be to all people. For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord.” Luke 2:10-11 KJV

Week 4: Light

“Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.” John 8:12 KJV

Christmas Day: the premiere

On December 25, 2026, gather the family to watch Episode 1 of A Light to Glory, “Let There Be Light.” Join the Founding Families for daily Advent reminders, and keep the habit going with our daily Bible verse.
