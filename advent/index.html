Advent with Pip & Lumi

A 30-day Advent devotional for families with teens and kids. It starts on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, November 26, 2026 and ends on Christmas Day with the premiere of A Light to Glory. Each day, open a door to find one KJV verse, one question for the table and one small thing to do.

The first door opens Thursday, Nov 26.

Printable calendar

How it works

  1. Read the day's KJV verse together. It takes about a minute.
  2. Ask the question around the table, in the car or over the group chat.
  3. Do the small action. Little lights add up.

Optional traditions: many families and churches light an Advent wreath on the four Sundays before Christmas (Nov 29, Dec 6, Dec 13 and Dec 20 this year). Others add a candle at dinner, a daily Scripture reading, fasting, or giving to people in need. Use whatever fits your family and your church; none of it is required here. Our weekly themes (Hope, Peace, Joy, Light) are a simple guide, and your church may use different ones.

The 30 doors

Doors unlock on their date (based on your device's clock). Parents can preview everything on the printable page.

Week of Hope

1Thu, Nov 26
“Arise, shine; for thy light is come, and the glory of the LORD is risen upon thee.” Isaiah 60:1 KJV

Ask: It's Thanksgiving: what's one thing you're thankful for this year?

Do: Light a candle (or flip on a lamp) at dinner and go around the table saying thanks.

2Fri, Nov 27
“The people that walked in darkness have seen a great light: they that dwell in the land of the shadow of death, upon them hath the light shined.” Isaiah 9:2 KJV

Ask: Where does the world feel dark right now?

Do: Text someone who's having a hard week.

3Sat, Nov 28
“I wait for the LORD, my soul doth wait, and in his word do I hope.” Psalm 130:5 KJV

Ask: What's something hard about waiting?

Do: Put your phone in another room for one hour.

4Sun, Nov 29
“Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.” Isaiah 7:14 KJV

Ask: Immanuel means "God with us." Where did you feel that today?

Do: Many churches begin Advent today. If your family uses a wreath, light the first candle.

5Mon, Nov 30
“But thou, Bethlehem Ephratah, though thou be little among the thousands of Judah, yet out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting.” Micah 5:2 KJV

Ask: Why would God choose a small town like Bethlehem?

Do: Notice someone who gets overlooked and include them.

6Tue, Dec 1
“Now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that ye may abound in hope, through the power of the Holy Ghost.” Romans 15:13 KJV

Ask: What are you hoping for this Christmas that isn't a thing?

Do: Write a hope on a sticky note and put it on the fridge.

7Wed, Dec 2
“But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.” Isaiah 40:31 KJV

Ask: When do you feel most tired, and what gives you strength?

Do: Go to bed 30 minutes early. Seriously.

Week of Peace

8Thu, Dec 3
“For unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given: and the government shall be upon his shoulder: and his name shall be called Wonderful, Counsellor, The mighty God, The everlasting Father, The Prince of Peace.” Isaiah 9:6 KJV

Ask: Which name for Jesus in this verse means the most to you right now?

Do: Make a peace offering: fix a small conflict at home.

9Fri, Dec 4
“Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid.” John 14:27 KJV

Ask: How is God's peace different from "everything going right"?

Do: Take five slow breaths together before a meal.

10Sat, Dec 5
“And the angel said unto her, Fear not, Mary: for thou hast found favour with God. And, behold, thou shalt conceive in thy womb, and bring forth a son, and shalt call his name JESUS.” Luke 1:30-31 KJV

Ask: Mary heard "Fear not." What would you want to hear those words about?

Do: Encourage someone who's facing something scary.

11Sun, Dec 6
“Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.” Isaiah 26:3 KJV

Ask: What fills your mind most often?

Do: Swap 15 minutes of scrolling for a walk or a game together.

12Mon, Dec 7
“And Mary said, Behold the handmaid of the Lord; be it unto me according to thy word. And the angel departed from her.” Luke 1:38 KJV

Ask: Mary said yes to something huge. What's God asking you to say yes to?

Do: Say yes to a chore without being asked.

13Tue, Dec 8
“And she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins.” Matthew 1:21 KJV

Ask: Why does the name Jesus matter?

Do: Look up what your own name means and share it.

14Wed, Dec 9
“And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.” Philippians 4:7 KJV

Ask: What would it feel like to have peace you can't fully explain?

Do: Pray for one person who doesn't have peace right now.

Week of Joy

15Thu, Dec 10
“And Mary said, My soul doth magnify the Lord, And my spirit hath rejoiced in God my Saviour.” Luke 1:46-47 KJV

Ask: Mary sang when she heard the news. What makes you want to sing?

Do: Play your family's favorite Christmas song loudly.

16Fri, Dec 11
“Thou wilt shew me the path of life: in thy presence is fulness of joy; at thy right hand there are pleasures for evermore.” Psalm 16:11 KJV

Ask: Where do you find real joy, not just fun?

Do: Make a "joy list" together and hang it up.

17Sat, Dec 12
“And the angel said unto them, Fear not: for, behold, I bring you good tidings of great joy, which shall be to all people. For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord.” Luke 2:10-11 KJV

Ask: Why was this "good tidings" for all people?

Do: Share good news with a neighbor: bake, draw or write something.

18Sun, Dec 13
“The LORD thy God in the midst of thee is mighty; he will save, he will rejoice over thee with joy; he will rest in his love, he will joy over thee with singing.” Zephaniah 3:17 KJV

Ask: How does it feel to know God rejoices over you?

Do: Tell each person at the table one thing you love about them.

19Mon, Dec 14
“This is the day which the LORD hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.” Psalm 118:24 KJV

Ask: What would make today a good day, even if nothing big happens?

Do: Do one thing just for fun as a family.

20Tue, Dec 15
“Through the tender mercy of our God; whereby the dayspring from on high hath visited us, To give light to them that sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, to guide our feet into the way of peace.” Luke 1:78-79 KJV

Ask: Who do you know who is sitting in darkness right now?

Do: Give away something you don't need to someone who does.

21Wed, Dec 16
“These things have I spoken unto you, that my joy might remain in you, and that your joy might be full.” John 15:11 KJV

Ask: What steals your joy most often?

Do: Write a thank-you note to a teacher, coach or pastor or priest.

Week of Light

22Thu, Dec 17
“Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.” John 8:12 KJV

Ask: What does it mean to follow the light?

Do: Take a night walk and look at Christmas lights together.

23Fri, Dec 18
“And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.” John 1:5 KJV

Ask: What hard thing is the darkness "not winning" against in your life?

Do: Light a candle at dinner and pray for someone by name.

24Sat, Dec 19
“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.” John 3:16 KJV

Ask: Why do you think God's love looks like a gift?

Do: Give a gift that costs time, not money.

25Sun, Dec 20
“In this was manifested the love of God toward us, because that God sent his only begotten Son into the world, that we might live through him.” 1 John 4:9 KJV

Ask: How did God show love in a way people could see?

Do: Show love in a way someone can see: help, hug or hang out.

26Mon, Dec 21
“Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid.” Matthew 5:14 KJV

Ask: Where can you shine this week?

Do: Do one secret kind act and don't tell anyone.

27Tue, Dec 22
“And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, (and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father,) full of grace and truth.” John 1:14 KJV

Ask: What does it mean that God "dwelt among us"?

Do: Spend time with someone who might be lonely this season.

28Wed, Dec 23
“For God, who commanded the light to shine out of darkness, hath shined in our hearts, to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.” 2 Corinthians 4:6 KJV

Ask: How has light changed something in your heart this month?

Do: Share your favorite Advent moment so far.

Christmas Eve & Christmas Day

29Thu, Dec 24
“And she brought forth her firstborn son, and wrapped him in swaddling clothes, and laid him in a manger; because there was no room for them in the inn.” Luke 2:7 KJV

Ask: There was no room at the inn. Who needs room made for them?

Do: Read Luke 2:1-20 together, whether at home or at church.

30Fri, Dec 25
“Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will toward men.” Luke 2:14 KJV

Ask: Merry Christmas! What's one way you'll keep the light going after today?

Do: Watch Episode 1 of A Light to Glory, "Let There Be Light," together.

Christmas Day: the premiere

On December 25, 2026, gather the family to watch Episode 1 of A Light to Glory, “Let There Be Light.” Parents and guardians (18+) can join the Founding Families for updates, and anyone can keep the habit going with today's Spark.
