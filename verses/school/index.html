Bible verses for school stress

Three King James Version verses to read, save or send to someone who needs them.

“If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.” James 1:5 KJV
“And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men;” Colossians 3:23 KJV
“Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established.” Proverbs 16:3 KJV

Lumi's take

Tests, deadlines, group projects and the pressure to be great at everything. God doesn't grade on a curve, and your worth isn't a GPA. Ask for wisdom (He promises to give it), do your honest best, and leave the results with Him.

Pray it

Lord, give me wisdom and focus today. Help me work hard and rest in You, whatever the grade. Amen.
