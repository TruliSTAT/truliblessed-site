Bible verses about identity

Three King James Version verses to read, save or send to someone who needs them.

“I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous are thy works; and that my soul knoweth right well.” Psalm 139:14 KJV
“For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them.” Ephesians 2:10 KJV
“But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light:” 1 Peter 2:9 KJV

Lumi's take

Filters, follower counts and comparison all try to tell you who you are. Scripture says you're God's workmanship. The Greek word is poiema, where we get "poem." You're not a draft. You were made on purpose, for a purpose.

Pray it

Creator, when I compare myself, remind me that I'm Your workmanship and wonderfully made. Amen.
