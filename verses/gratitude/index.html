Bible verses about gratitude

Three King James Version verses to read, save or send to someone who needs them.

“In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you.” 1 Thessalonians 5:18 KJV
“O give thanks unto the LORD, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.” Psalm 107:1 KJV
“And whatsoever ye do in word or deed, do all in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God and the Father by him.” Colossians 3:17 KJV

Lumi's take

Notice it says "in every thing," not "for every thing." You don't have to be thankful for a bad day, but you can find something good inside one. Try it tonight: name three things, however small. Gratitude is a muscle.

Pray it

Father, thank You for the good I noticed today and the good I missed. Teach me to see it. Amen.
