Bible verses about fear

Three King James Version verses to read, save or send to someone who needs them.

“Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.” Isaiah 41:10 KJV
“What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee.” Psalm 56:3 KJV
“For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.” 2 Timothy 1:7 KJV

Lumi's take

Notice that David doesn't say "if" I am afraid. He says "what time," meaning when. Fear isn't a sign that your faith failed. It's the moment faith gets to do something. Courage isn't the absence of fear. It's trusting God while your hands are still shaking.

Pray it

Lord, when fear shows up, I choose to trust You. Hold me up and help me take the next step. Amen.
