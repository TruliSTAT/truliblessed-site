Bible verses for anxiety

Three King James Version verses to read, save or send to someone who needs them.

“Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.” Philippians 4:6-7 KJV
“Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.” 1 Peter 5:7 KJV
“Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself. Sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof.” Matthew 6:34 KJV

Lumi's take

Anxiety is loud. It narrates every worst case like it's a movie trailer. Scripture doesn't tell you to pretend it isn't there. It tells you where to take it. Paul wrote about peace from a prison cell, so he wasn't writing from an easy life. Try this: name the worry, pray it in one honest sentence, then do the next small thing.

Pray it

God, here's what's weighing on me. I'm handing it to You, one worry at a time. Guard my heart and mind with Your peace. Amen.
