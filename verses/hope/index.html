Bible verses about hope

Three King James Version verses to read, save or send to someone who needs them.

“For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the LORD, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end.” Jeremiah 29:11 KJV
“Now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that ye may abound in hope, through the power of the Holy Ghost.” Romans 15:13 KJV
“But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.” Isaiah 40:31 KJV

Lumi's take

Jeremiah 29:11 was first written to people in exile, a long way from home, who were told to settle in for seventy years. Hope in Scripture isn't wishful thinking. It's confidence that God is still writing the story when you're stuck in a hard chapter.

Pray it

God of hope, fill me with joy and peace today, and help me trust that You're not finished with my story. Amen.
