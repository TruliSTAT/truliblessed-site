Bible verses for sleep

Three King James Version verses to read, save or send to someone who needs them.

“I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety.” Psalm 4:8 KJV
“When thou liest down, thou shalt not be afraid: yea, thou shalt lie down, and thy sleep shall be sweet.” Proverbs 3:24 KJV
“Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.” Matthew 11:28 KJV

Lumi's take

Racing thoughts at midnight? You're in good company, because David wrote some of his best Psalms on hard nights. Put the phone down, breathe slowly, and pray one of these verses back to God. You can stop holding the whole world up now.

Pray it

Lord, I lay down today's worries. Give me peace and sweet sleep, and watch over the people I love. Amen.
