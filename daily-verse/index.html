Daily Bible verse for teens (and the grown-ups too)

One verse. One minute. Into the light. Every day Truli Blessed shares a KJV verse of the day with a short line of Christian motivation. Here is a week of favorites to start with. Follow @truliblessedim on Instagram or X for a new one each morning.

Monday: when you feel too young to matter

“Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.” 1 Timothy 4:12 KJV

Tuesday: when you are scared

“Have not I commanded thee? Be strong and of a good courage; be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed: for the LORD thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest.” Joshua 1:9 KJV

Wednesday: when you don’t know what’s next

“Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.” Proverbs 3:5-6 KJV

Thursday: Christian motivation for a hard day

“I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me.” Philippians 4:13 KJV

Friday: when it feels dark

“And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.” John 1:5 KJV

Saturday: follow the light

“Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.” John 8:12 KJV

Sunday: let it shine

“Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.” Matthew 5:16 KJV
